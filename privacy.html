Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your personal data, in line with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019.

Effective date: 16 November 2026Operator: Jamafriq Global Consultancy, a business registered in the Republic of KenyaContact: jmsorwa4@gmail.com · WhatsApp +254 721 776 745

1. Data controller

Jamafriq Global Consultancy, Nairobi, Kenya, is the data controller for personal data collected through this Site and our services. Contact: {EMAIL}.

2. What we collect

  • Contact details you give us: name, phone or WhatsApp number, email address.
  • Enquiry and application details: the course or service you are interested in, target band, destination country, start date, referral source and anything you write in a message.
  • Student records once you enrol: attendance, mock scores, feedback and payment records.
  • Placement documents you choose to share: academic transcripts, CV, passport copy and similar, used only for the application you ask us to support.
  • Mentorship registrations: participant name and age range, and the registering parent or guardian's contact details.
  • Reviews you submit, including your name and band score if provided.
  • Technical data: the forms on this Site do not send data to our servers; they prepare a WhatsApp message that you choose to send. Our hosting provider records standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages requested) for security. We do not currently run analytics; if we add it, it will only load after you choose “Accept all” in the cookie banner.
  • Marketing preference: whether you ticked the optional box to receive WhatsApp updates. Unticked means we will not send you marketing.

Please do not send ID numbers, passport numbers or medical details through the website forms. When a placement needs documents, we will agree a secure channel with you.

3. Why we use it (lawful basis)

  • To respond to your enquiry and deliver the service you request (performance of a contract).
  • To issue receipts and keep accounting records (legal obligation).
  • To publish reviews you submit, and to send you updates about intakes and offers only if you ticked the optional updates box (consent, which you may withdraw at any time by replying STOP or writing to us).
  • To improve the Site and our services (legitimate interests, balanced against your rights).

4. Sharing

We share personal data only as needed to deliver your service: with the institution, employer, recruiter or visa partner you apply to; with IDP IELTS for test-related matters; with Safaricom (M-Pesa) when you pay us. We never sell personal data.

Services this website uses

  • Vercel Inc. (USA): hosts the website and keeps short-term server logs.
  • WhatsApp (Meta Platforms): carries the messages you choose to send from our forms and links.
  • Safaricom M-Pesa: processes payments for mentorship sessions and services.
  • Gmail (Google): receives email you send to {EMAIL}.
  • Facebook, Instagram and X: only if you click our social links; nothing from them loads on the Site.

Fonts and images are served from our own website. The Site loads no advertising, tracking pixels, maps or video embeds.

5. International transfers

Placement applications necessarily involve sending your data to institutions and employers outside Kenya (for example in Canada, the UK, the USA, Australia, Germany or Poland). We do this only for the applications you ask us to make, and we tell you which organisation receives your documents.

6. Retention

Enquiries that do not become a service are deleted after 12 months. Student and placement records are kept for 5 years after the service ends, as required for accounting and to support later references or re-applications, then deleted. Reviews stay published until you ask us to remove them.

7. Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act, 2019 you may ask to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your data, object to or restrict processing, and withdraw consent. Write to {EMAIL}; we respond within 30 days. You may also complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) of Kenya.

8. Security

Documents are stored in access-controlled cloud storage and shared only with the recipients named above. We use HTTPS on this Site. No method of transmission is completely secure, so please share sensitive documents through the channels we confirm with you rather than public platforms.

9. Children

The Mentorship Programme serves participants aged 15 to 24. For participants under 18 we collect data only through a parent or guardian, who may exercise the rights above on the participant's behalf.

10. Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above shows the current version.
