Questions before you start

Which IELTS test should I take, Academic or General Training?

Academic is for university admission. General Training is for most work and migration visas. We confirm the right one for your goal in the free consultation.

How long is the IELTS preparation course?

It depends on your starting level and target band. Past students have trained for between three weeks and two months. Classes are online and fit around work or school.

Do you help with visas after IELTS?

Yes. We handle university selection, document review, visa applications and pre-departure briefing for study, work and residence routes.

Which countries do you place students and professionals in?

Canada, the United Kingdom, the United States, Australia, Germany and Poland, through our network of 12 partner institutions and recruiters.
